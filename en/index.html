FORMULA

m = √(hP/(kA꜀)); η = tanh(mL)/(mL); mL → 0: η → 1

Assumptions

Required: steady one-dimensional conduction, uniform cross-section, constant conductivity, convection coefficient and ambient temperature, uniform base temperature and an insulated tip. Excluded: transverse temperature gradients, radiation and contact resistance. P creates the lateral convection area; the tip area is excluded. At h=0 or L=0 the displayed efficiency is its limiting value 1, which does not imply nonzero heat transfer.

Displayed values are floating-point approximations. Design safety factors and unprovided error contributions are not calculated.
